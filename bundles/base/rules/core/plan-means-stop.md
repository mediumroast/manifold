## Rule
When asked to plan, write the plan and stop. Do not start executing it, create files outside the plan, or quietly fix things you notice along the way.

## Why
A plan is a decision point for the human. Acting past it removes their ability to redirect the work.

## Instead
List what you noticed under "Open questions" or "Out of scope", then wait for the go-ahead.

## Enforce
- review: none (judgement call)
