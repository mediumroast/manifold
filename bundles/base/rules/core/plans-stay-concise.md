## Rule
Keep plans short and complete: the problem, the change, the checkable tasks. Do not pad them with background the reader already has or with sections the template does not call for.

## Why
Padding costs the reader time and tokens on every read, and makes the missing piece harder to spot.

## Instead
Link to the existing document instead of restating it. Prefer fewer, complete tasks over many vague ones.

## Enforce
- review: every section in the plan is in the template and has content
