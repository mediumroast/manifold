## Rule
If a requirement, name, data shape or acceptance criterion is unclear, ask before you proceed. Do not plan or code around it with TODOs or vague wording.

## Why
Ambiguity resolved by guessing turns into rework.

## Instead
Ask one focused question, or write a recommendation under "Open questions" and wait for the answer. Every ask states, in plain words, what is asked, why, the consequence and the default, and never names an item only by an internal ID (a task, checkpoint, finding or open-question number) without saying what it is.
